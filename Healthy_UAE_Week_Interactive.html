Hydration Challenge

الاثنين
تحدي شرب الماء
Did you know?
Your body is about 60% water. In the UAE heat you lose water fast through sweat, so drink before you feel thirsty.
Signs your body needs water
Headache Tiredness Dry lips Dizziness Poor focus

My Healthy Snack

الأربعاء
وجبتي الخفيفة الصحية
Make a simple healthy snack and explain what is in it. A healthy snack uses at least two food groups and has little added sugar or salt.
The food groups
Food groupExamplesWhat it does for you
Fruits and vegetablesDates, apples, carrots, cucumberVitamins, fibre, healthy digestion
GrainsWholemeal bread, oats, brown rice crackersEnergy for the day
ProteinEggs, labneh, hummus, nuts, chickenBuilds and repairs muscles
DairyLaban, yoghurt, milk, cheeseStrong bones and teeth
Healthy fatsOlive oil, avocado, seedsBrain and heart health
Snack ideas (no cooking needed)
Wholemeal bread, labneh, cucumber and za'atar
Fruit salad with yoghurt and a few nuts
Carrot and cucumber sticks with hummus
Two dates with a glass of laban
